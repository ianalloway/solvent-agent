🪙 SOLVENT: an AI agent that runs its own research business

SOLVENT quotes research jobs against its own costs, declines the unprofitable ones (offering a counter-offer instead), and books every job into a treasury P&L. This page runs the real solvent-agent package inside your browser with Pyodide, in offline demo mode: simulated Stripe checkout, deterministic stub analyst, no API keys, no server.
